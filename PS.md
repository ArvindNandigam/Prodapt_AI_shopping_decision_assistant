# AI shopping decision assistant

Choosing the right product among many options can be confusing.

Build an AI shopping assistant that helps users compare products based on their needs, budget and preferences while explaining advantages disadvantages and recommendations.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

1. Problem Understanding, Approach & Innovation

    - Understanding of the problem statement, identification of user needs, assumptions, solution approach, creativity, and ability to introduce innovative ideas using AI/LLM capabilities.

2. Prompt Engineering & LLM Utilization

    - Ability to design effective prompts, select and leverage LLM capabilities, understand model limitations, optimize responses, and integrate LLMs effectively into the solution.

3. Data Modeling & Database Design

    - Ability to design data models, define entities and relationships, select appropriate databases, design schemas, and demonstrate effective data storage and retrieval strategies.

4. Solution Architecture, Code Flow & Structure

    - Quality of architecture design, application flow, code organization, modularity, readability, maintainability, and ability to explain technical implementation decisions.

5. Technology Stack Selection & Technical Reasoning

    - Selection of technologies, frameworks, and tools with clear justification based on business requirements, scalability, performance, and maintainability considerations.

6. Requirements Analysis & Technical Documentation (BRD/HLD)

    - Ability to translate business requirements into structured documentation, define system components, workflows, architecture decisions, and technical design.

7. Scalability, Performance, Testing & Quality

    - Consideration of scalability, performance optimization, error handling, edge cases, testing approach, validation strategy, and overall solution reliability.

8. Security, PII Management & Data Privacy

    - Understanding and implementation of security best practices, PII handling, privacy controls, access management, and secure data processing.

9. Demo, Communication & Solution Walkthrough

    - Effectiveness in demonstrating the solution, explaining workflows, presenting technical decisions, answering questions, and communicating the implementation clearly.

10. Business Value & Impact

    - Ability to articulate the business problem, solution benefits, user impact, adoption potential, ROI/value proposition, and future opportunities.
